Phone on the platter: the visual turns counter-clockwise so it appears to stand still. Auto (gyro) follows the real platter speed. Auto-locate: if the phone is not centred on the spindle, press Start with the platter stopped, then switch the turntable on; the sensors find the spindle and the picture stays fixed in the room (readout at top left). Spin around: “Screen centre” keeps the picture still when the spindle is under the screen centre; “Visual centre” just spins the picture on its own centre.
